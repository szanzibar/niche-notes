# niche-notes — v1 spec

## Problem

Amplenote is a paid subscription, its sync has lost data, and it is cluttered. We want a scratch pad for a family of two or three: pop open a phone, jot a note or a list, find it later, and edit the same shopping list together.

## What v1 does

- **Notes in a tree.** Any note can hold notes. There are no folders and no notebooks. New notes go on top. Every row has a + for a note inside it. Drag a note onto the middle of another to nest it, onto its top or bottom edge to sit next to it, or onto the header to bring it back to the top. A line shows where it will land, indented the way it will be. On a touch screen every row has a grip that drags at once; anywhere else a long press does it. Back from a note returns to the tree, even when a widget opened the note, and back again leaves the app.
- **Pins.** A pinned note sits above its siblings, at the top level or inside its parent. Pins are ordered among themselves by dragging, and a note dropped among pinned notes is pinned too.
- **Search.** The box at the top of the tree filters as you type, title matches first, then text, each with the words around the hit. Trashed notes are included and marked. Arrow keys and Enter open a result on a keyboard. It works offline, since every note is on the device. The box always shows a clear button while it has text.
- **One editor.** A title field and a body with three heading levels, code blocks, quotes, dividers, bold, italic, strikethrough, inline code, links, highlight, bullet lists, numbered lists and checkbox lists. Two lists of one kind that touch are one list, so only a blank line keeps them apart. A checked item is crossed out. Ticking a box never moves the note: while typing, the cursor goes to the end of that line, and otherwise the keyboard stays down. In a note set to hide them, it hides once it and every item under it are checked, for everyone and in its widgets. Checked items wait until the note has been still for a moment, then fold away together, so ticking down a list never moves the next box. A note set to delete them hides them the same way and deletes them the next time it opens. A checkbox button beside the title opens Hide checked, Delete when checked, Uncheck all and Delete checked in a box under it, and a tap anywhere else closes it. Every box a button opens floats beside that button, over the note. Uncheck all clears every box, so a packing list can be used again. Delete checked removes the items that would hide, showing them for a moment first when they are hidden. A tap on a link puts the cursor in it, and its address above the toolbar opens it. Dragging a line by its handle moves it, with everything nested under it. A tap toolbar does all of this on every platform, and markdown shortcuts work too (`- `, `1. `, `[] `, `# `, `> `, `---`). Opening a note puts the cursor in it: an empty title first, otherwise where this device last left the cursor, or the end of the text. Enter or Tab moves from the title into the body. Undo history stays with the note while the app is open.
- **Dates and alerts.** A checkbox line can have a date, a time and a repeat. The date button in the toolbar sets them in a box that stays open until a tap elsewhere, and so does a tap on the date at the end of the line. Today, Tomorrow and Next week are one tap. A line repeats every week on chosen weekdays (all seven is every day), every month on a day of the month or on the 1st to 4th or last of chosen weekdays, or every year, each every one or more weeks, months or years apart, and the box says the rule in words. The box shows the weekday beside the date, and its weekdays start on Monday. Dates and times follow the device's language settings. The date reads Today, Tomorrow, a weekday within the week, or the date itself, and turns the accent color once it is overdue. Ticking a repeating line moves it to its next date instead of checking it. When a line comes due, everyone on the note gets an alert at its time, or at 9:00 when it has none, in each device's own time zone. The alert's Done ticks the line, Snooze 1 hour moves it to an hour later, and a tap opens the note at the line. Settings says whether alerts are on for the device. It also adds a calendar that shows every dated line, and each event links to its line in the note. On a computer, one button opens Google Calendar's page for adding it, one hands it to a calendar app, and one copies the link. On Android, Copy link gives Google's link to paste into a browser, since Google Calendar's app can't add it. A phone shows the calendar once Sync is on for it in the Calendar app. Google refreshes it about once a day.
- **Private by default.** A note is visible only to its owner until the owner shares it with a specific person. Sharing grants full edit access to that note only, not to its children.
- **Your tree is yours.** Sharing a note does not move it. It appears at the top of the other person's tree, under their pins, and they drag it wherever they like. A note someone else owns shows their name wherever it appears, and its header says who shared it instead of offering Share, and a Leave button in place of Delete moves it to their own trash, where Restore brings it back and Leave for good drops the share for them alone. The owner's note is never touched.
- **Offline first.** Everything except sharing works without a connection: create, edit, move and delete, including notes shared with you. Changes merge when the connection returns. A crossed-out cloud in the header says when the server can't be reached.
- **Real time.** Two people editing one note see each other's keystrokes, and each other's cursor with a name on it.
- **Trash, not confirmations.** Delete is one tap. The note goes to a Trash section at the bottom of the tree, where it can be read but not edited, restored, or deleted forever. Anything in the trash for thirty days is deleted for good, by the client the next time it starts, so no server job is involved.
- **Settings.** A highlight color from a palette, light, dark or follow-the-device appearance, and a compact, default or large density. The color and appearance follow the user to every device, and density stays with each device, since a phone and a desktop want different sizes.
- **Note header.** The actions sit beside the title until it would be cut off. Then they all fold behind dots that reveal them under the title, and the title ends in "…". A people icon when the note is shared either way, and the crossed-out cloud when offline, stay beside the dots.
- **Widgets.** On Android, any number of home-screen widgets, each showing a whole note you pick, read-only. Adding or reconfiguring a widget opens the tree to pick from, and tapping the widget opens its note in the app. Widgets refresh every 30 minutes and from a refresh button.
- **Native.** An Android app and the web app, which Chrome and Edge install as a desktop app, all loading the same build from the server. The Android app updates itself from the project's releases: it downloads a newer one in the background and offers to install it on the next launch.
- **Self-hosted.** One Docker container and one SQLite file. Login through Pocket ID passkeys, and a session lasts a year past the last launch. A person can be shared with once they have logged in.

## Not in v1

Markdown export, sharing a note with its children, images and attachments, iOS, view-only sharing.
